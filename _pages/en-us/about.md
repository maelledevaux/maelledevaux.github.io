`Femtomagnetism` is the field of study concerned with the dynamics of magnetization on the femtosecond timescale induced by an ultrashort laser pulse. Éric Beaurepaire and his colleagues introduced this field of research in 1996 at the IPCMS, which led to the creation of the [FEMTOMAG](https://www.ipcms.fr/en/equipe/femtomagnetism-and-spin-dynamics-femtomag-team/) team, where I am pursuing my Ph.D.

My research lies at the intersection of condensed matter physics and ultrafast optics. In particular, I focus on studying the `ultrashort dynamics` of the Néel vector in $$\ce{FePS3}$$, a compound that exhibits antiferromagnetic order on a 2D scale and displays unique magneto-optical properties.

I am a `teaching assistant` in several departments at the University of Strasbourg.
